# Research Sponsered Projects

##  Keyword Spotting for Indian Languages  
**National Language Translation Mission (NLTM), MeitY, India**  
*June 2022 – Present*  
**Institute:** Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IITH)  
**Advisor:** Prof. K. Sri Rama Murty  

- Developed **lattice-based** and **lattice-free keyword spotting (KWS)** systems for **13 Indian languages**, including Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, and Bengali.  
- Designed scalable **multilingual KWS pipelines** to support cross-language keyword detection.  

---

## Robot Audition  
**Defence Research and Development Organisation (DRDO), Hyderabad, India**  
*January 2022 – Present*  
**Institute:** Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IITH)  

- Developed and integrated **core audio modules** for a humanoid **robot audition system**.  
- Built **wake-word recognition**, **command recognition**, and **speech-based interaction** modules for **English, Hindi, Urdu, and Mandarin**.  
- Designed both **lattice-based** and **end-to-end neural keyword spotting systems**.  
- Implemented **multilingual ASR** using **Whisper** models for real-time robot interaction.

## Automatic Speech Recognition for Indian English  
**Collaboration with I’m Beside You, Japan**  
*June 2022 – June 2023*  
**Institute:** IIT Hyderabad  

- Developed an **ASR system for Indian English** using **self-supervised learning models (Wav2Vec2)**.  
- Enhanced recognition accuracy across **Indian speakers** with diverse pronunciations.
