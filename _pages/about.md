👋 Hi, I'm **Ramesh Gundluru**, a **Research Scholar** at the **Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IITH)**, specializing in **Speech and Audio Processing**, **Machine Learning**, and **Deep Learning**.  

---

## 🎓 Education & Research Journey

My academic journey began at the Rajiv Gandhi University of Knowledge Technologies (RGUKT), RK Valley, where I earned my Bachelor of Technology (B.Tech) in Electronics and Communication Engineering with a CGPA of 9.08/10. In my final years of undergraduate study, I was fortunate to receive an opportunity to work as an intern under the guidance of Prof. K. Sri Rama Murty at the Speech Information Processing Laboratory (SIPLab), Indian Institute of Technology Hyderabad (IITH). There, I worked on the recognition of paralinguistic aspects of speech, such as emotion and language information. This experience sparked my passion for speech and audio research, inspiring me to pursue advanced studies in the field.

Motivated by this experience, I joined IIT Hyderabad for my M.Tech in Electrical Engineering. During my Master’s program, I gained in-depth knowledge of speech signal processing and machine learning. I learned to build complete speech systems — including automatic speech recognition (ASR), text speech synthesis, and keyword spotting. These projects helped me understand the end-to-end pipeline of modern speech technology — from feature extraction and acoustic modeling to language modeling and system evaluation. This hands-on experience strengthened both my technical expertise and my curiosity to explore AI for speech.  

I am deeply inspired by Prof. K. Sri Rama Murty, whose remarkable expertise in speech and audio signal processing, pattern recognition, machine learning and deep learning has greatly influenced my research journey. His insightful guidance, clarity of thought, and problem-solving approach ignited my passion for exploring in speech signal processing.
It motivated me to continue my journey as an Integrated Ph.D. scholar under his supervision at the same institute.

My research focuses on developing unified neural architectures for voice search, aiming to make human-machine interaction more natural and accessible. I am passionate about building intelligent, real-time, and multilingual speech systems, and I’m now seeking roles where I can contribute to speech AI research, or voice-enabled technology development in cutting-edge organizations.
